Welcome to the Ministry Video Toolkit

You record yourself and write a script. Claude turns them into a finished ministry update video, with your photos, titles, music and an end screen. You don't need to know anything about video editing.

Not sure what to do next? Just ask Claude. Open Claude Code in the toolkit folder and say what you'd like, in your own words. Claude does the technical parts for you.

How it works

  1. See what you can make

    Watch an example made with this toolkit (3 minutes).

    Watch the example video
  2. Write your script

    Write what you'll say, with each picture placed just before the words it goes with. The example script gives you ideas for how to tell Claude to edit your video:

    Copy the Google Doc Notion version
  3. Record yourself

    Read your script to the camera. Don't worry about pauses or false starts: Claude trims them.

  4. Let Claude hear your wordsHighly recommended

    With a free transcription key, Claude knows exactly when you say each word, so every photo and title appears right on time. It takes just a few moments. Ask Claude: “Help me set up transcription.”

  5. Give Claude your recording and script

    Say something like this:

    I've recorded a ministry update. The video is at C:\Videos\update.mp4 and the script is at [link to your Google Doc or Notion page]. Make the video.
  6. Approve the storyboard

    Claude makes a page showing every scene with its words and pictures. Change anything you like and leave notes. Then tell Claude you're done, and Claude makes your changes.

  7. Add photos, music and video clipsOptional
    • Photos: use your own ministry photos, photos from related work like a Bible dedication (with the owner's OK), or free photos Claude finds online. Each photo's credit shows on screen.
    • Music: free music Claude finds for you to choose, or, with an ElevenLabs account, music made for your video.
    • Video clips: free clips for moments with no photos, like "the whole world." A free Pixabay account gives many more.

    Just ask: “Can you add music?”, “Add this photo of mine here.” or “Can you find a video clip for this part?”

  8. Review the video

    Claude makes the video and a review page. Watch it and leave notes on anything you'd like changed, then tell Claude you're done. Claude makes a new version until it looks right.

  9. Finish and share

    Claude makes the final video, a thumbnail for YouTube, a suggested description, and captions. Just ask for any of them.

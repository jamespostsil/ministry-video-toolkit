# Example media notice

The example video's footage, photos and end-frame images (downloaded by
`npm run example:media`, and the smaller copies in `examples/script-images/`)
come from a real, published ministry update by James and Christina Post with
Wycliffe Bible Translators.

**They are not covered by this repository's MIT licence.** They are shared so
you can see and learn how the toolkit works. Please don't reuse them in your own
videos or publications.

- **Scripture dedication photos** belong to the organisations and photographers
  credited in the video's captions: Wycliffe, SIL PNG, BTL, Peris Kiriko and
  Chris Gilmore.
- **The communications samples** are published Wycliffe Bible Translators USA
  materials.
- **The Wycliffe logo** belongs to Wycliffe Bible Translators and may only be
  used as its brand guidelines allow.
- **Family photos, footage and the end-frame photo** belong to James and
  Christina Post.
- **The music** in the published video is "Ambient - Ambient Music" by paulyudin
  on [Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/music/ambient-ambient-ambient-music-574003/).
  It isn't included here, because Pixabay's licence doesn't allow redistributing
  the file. Download it yourself and `npm run example:media -- --music <file>`
  builds the video's music bed from it; until then a generated stand-in plays.

To make your own video, use your own footage and photos, or ones you have
permission to use.
